**1 Peter 2:2-3 (NLT)**
> 2 Like newborn babies, you must crave spiritual milk so that you will grow into a full experience of salvation. Cry out for this nourishment, 3 now that have had a taste of the Lord's kindness.

---

I love this analogy because of both the necessity for milk with newborn babies for growth, but also the simplicity of milk and how that relates to the gospel. Babies can only feed on milk in
infancy (as they grow, they obviously progress to solid foods) and it's the same for us as baby believers. It's ESSENTIAL that we feed on the simple truths of the gospel before we can advance to
the spiritual meat of deepened theology, furthered comprehension of God's character/nature, wiser navigation of relationships within the Church (e.g., 1 Corinthians 8), and far more. However, unlike
developing babies, this nourishment from the pure milk doesn't become inadequate or unfulfilling as we grow... in fact it seems that it continues to get even better! I understand the intricacies of
the gospel so much more in depth than when I first came to know the Lord, but the simplicity of the Good News never ceases to satisfy my heart the same way as it did when I was a child.
