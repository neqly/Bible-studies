**Jude 1:24 (NLT)**
> 24 Now all glory to God, who is able to keep you from falling away and will bring you with great joy into his glorious presence.

---

"who is able to keep you from falling away..." This is yet another verse proving you can't lose your salvation, even though it's already abundantly clear. If you could lose your salvation,
then 1. that's not good news at all because you have to work to keep it and 2. It would be God's fault that you lost it since that belief infers He's simply "trying to best" to save you.
